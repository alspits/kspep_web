# Evidence synthesis

Sources: raw-evidence.md E1–E6, captured through browser screenshots, accessibility trees and DOM inspection. Three evidence reviewers examined structure/friction, visual presentation, and copy/honesty; synthesis below incorporates later E6 verification.

1. Innovative: no comparative peer research. A familiar portrait-and-text landing pattern is visible (E1); originality cannot be established.
2. Useful: messaging links support contact; mobile CTA begins at y=1400; price appears in late FAQ (E1, E3, E4).
3. Aesthetic: coherent cream/navy base; mixed Roboto/Manrope; circular decorative photos; mobile logo overlaps text (E1, E5, E6).
4. Understandable: concrete concerns and labels, but no section navigation, late price, messenger changes by section, logo unexpectedly links to formats (E2–E4).
5. Unobtrusive: persistent logo overlaps mobile paragraph; two desktop sections each consume 900px with quote/decorative content (E2, E6).
6. Honest: qualified duration, no guaranteed outcome or fake scarcity observed, price and conditions provided (E3). Credentials are claims supported by displayed documents, not independently authenticated (E6).
7. Long-lasting: simple palette and portrait survive a redesign; reduced decoration and consistent typography would improve durability (E1, E5; design judgment).
8. Thorough: main headings lack H2/H3 structure; logo destination and overlap are concrete rough edges. Full interaction-state audit not performed (E2, E4–E6).
9. Resource conscious: 12977px desktop /16901px mobile; repeated copy imposes attention cost. No performance trace, byte count, request count or TTI measured (E2, E5).
10. Essential: repeated reassurances, two approach statements and full-height decorations can be consolidated without losing the offer (E2, E3).

Gaps: no complete source tree, unused code analysis, exhaustive strings inventory, exact visible-token count, keyboard test, performance benchmark, analytics or conversion experiment. Do not interpret unverified states as missing states.
